# Visual bible

## Style chung

2D anime illustration in the style of a Vox explainer video: clean cel-shaded line art, flat saturated colors, subtle halftone dots and paper-grain texture, editorial infographic composition, cut-out collage layering, warm cream paper background, limited palette of ink navy, vermilion red, imperial gold and jade teal, soft paper-cutout drop shadows, cinematic 16:9 framing, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark

## Mio trong tranh

Mio, a young anime girl guide with short teal bob hair, round glasses, mustard-yellow jacket and carrying a small paper scroll

Giữ cùng gương mặt, tóc teal ngắn, kính tròn, áo mustard. Mio là dẫn giải hiện đại hư cấu, không phải nhân vật lịch sử. Không thêm Mio ở cảnh không gọi nhân vật. Mascot kênh Trà Xanh là lớp dựng riêng nếu được yêu cầu về sau.

## Motif

- MIO: Mio, a young anime girl guide with short teal bob hair, round glasses, mustard-yellow jacket and carrying a small paper scroll
- NOTE: an unlettered historical paper banknote with an abstract ornamental border, not a modern dollar bill
- SILVER: silver bullion ingots or silver coins, used as an alternative store of value
- TRUSTGAUGE: an abstract editorial trust gauge with a simple needle and colored arc, no ticks or numerals; this is a visual metaphor, not measured data
- TAXGATE: a stylized tax-office gateway with collectors and incoming payments, an editorial symbol of fiscal demand
- WARDRUM: a large military drum symbolizing wartime expenditure
- PRESS: a period-appropriate money-printing workshop; in Chinese historical scenes use woodblock printing tables, brushes, ink and paper, never a European movable-type machine
- PRICEBOARD: an abstract goods-and-purchasing-power diagram with increasing stacks of notes beside constant quantities of goods, no text, numerals or purported statistical values

## QA
