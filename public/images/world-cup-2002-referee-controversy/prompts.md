# Prompt set — World Cup 2002

**Base style used for every image:** Vox-style 2D anime editorial illustration; bold clean outlines; flat vibrant colors; minimal vector shading; soft cel shading; expressive faces; subtle grain; vertical 3:4. Keep the main action centered and leave the lower/far-right area relatively clean for Shorts captions. No readable text, numbers, logos, flags, watermarks, badges, signage, or real-person likenesses. Controversial refereeing is framed as satirical fan-perspective debate, not as proof of a factual claim.

1. A packed 2002-style stadium, red-clad home supporters, a floating referee whistle and comic question mark: a jubilant yet suspicious tournament hook.
2. The host team enters a football stadium staged like a theatre, caught in an enormous spotlight while their opponents wait in cooler shadow: a metaphor for host-nation spectacle.
3. A blue-kit player falls near a red-kit opponent while a neutral referee shows a red card; split supporters and replay-camera imagery communicate ambiguity.
4. A red-kit player heads a cross to goal; an uncertain glowing line, conflicting replay angles, celebration and protest make the aerial goal a debate.
5. A white-kit player celebrates near the net as a referee stops play; an abstract replay screen and stunned fans depict a disputed disallowed goal.
6. A red-kit player takes a penalty as the goalkeeper dives and a split crowd reacts: a high-energy shootout climax.
7. Eleven red-kit players and one oversized referee silhouette casting a long shadow: a clearly symbolic, comic “twelve versus eleven” supporter metaphor.
8. A black-and-white-kit player duels a determined red-kit defender under floodlights, with the referee far away: a restrained, genuine semifinal contest.
9. Red-kit players and a huge crowd celebrate under fireworks as though they won everything, with a subtle fourth-step podium in the distance.
10. Anonymous football officials sit at microphones amid journalists and multiple replay panels showing different ambiguous angles: a post-tournament refereeing debate.
11. A football hovers above a globe-shaped stadium; supporters celebrate on one side while critics analyse replays on the other: a divided legacy.
12. In a twilight stadium, a justice scale holds a football and referee whistle and tilts uncertainly: a reflective final image about relative fairness.
